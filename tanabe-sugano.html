Conventions
  • All inputs are in units of B, except for the AOM values, which are entered in cm−1. Ratios between parameters (Dq, Ds, Dt) are unitless.
  • For the electron-repulsion terms, the Slater–Condon parameter F0 (part of Racah A) shifts every state of a given dn configuration by the same amount, so it is dropped (B = F2 − 5F4, C = 35F4). If you want the full Racah expressions, you will need to add this term back in; for plotting purposes it is essentially just an offset, so leaving it out makes things easier.
  • This way of calculating energies does not include spin–orbit coupling. SOC makes these calculations substantially more difficult, so although I might implement it at some point, it is not currently included.
  • Condon–Shortley phases are used for the complex d orbitals, so keep this in mind if some sign conventions appear unusual.
  • One-electron energies are measured from the barycenter: b1g (x2−y2) 6Dq + 2Ds − Dt, a1g (z2) 6Dq − 2Ds − 6Dt, b2g (xy) −4Dq + 2Ds − Dt, eg (xz, yz) −4Dq − Ds + 4Dt. Positive Ds and Dt correspond to weaker axial ligands (elongation along z); some texts use the opposite sign of Ds.
  • States are labeled 2S+1Γ in D4h and numbered (k) in order of energy when a label repeats. The blocks come from ML mod 4 (0: A1g/A2g; 2: B1g/B2g; ±1: Eg), A1/A2 and B1/B2 from the C2′(x) character, and S from ⟨S2⟩. Every state is gerade.
  • Each state is shown with its leading strong-field configuration (b2g, eg, a1g, b1g occupations; t2g, eg on the Oh cut) and its weight.

1 Energy levels along a cut

Angular overlap model: trans-MA4L2 → Dq, Ds, Dt

Four equatorial ligands A on ±x, ±y, two axial ligands L on ±z; δ = e(eq) − e(ax). 10Dq = 3eσ(eq) − 4eπ(eq), Ds = (2/7)(δσ + δπ), Dt = (2/35)(3δσ − 4δπ). Weaker axial ligands (δ > 0) give Ds, Dt > 0.

Loading.

Energies in units of B, with C/B as set and F0 dropped. Along the cut Ds = (Ds/Dq)·Dq and Dt = (Dt/Dq)·Dq; Ds, Dt > 0 means weaker axial ligands (elongation along z). Each curve is the k-th state of one (2S+1, Γ) block of D4h; at Ds = Dt = 0 the curves are the Oh levels. Color marks the spin multiplicity. The correlation diagram holds Dq fixed and runs (Ds, Dt) = τ·(right-end values) from τ = −1 through the Oh point τ = 0 to τ = 1. In cm−1 mode every energy is multiplied by B, here and in sections 2 and 3; the inputs stay in units of B. The CSV holds the curves shown, in the units shown.

2 Energy surfaces over a plane

dn and C/B are taken from section 1.

Compute the surfaces to list them.

Rendered views (Manim)

Each surface is the k-th state of one (2S+1, Γ) block of D4h, so it keeps its identity over the whole plane: surfaces of different blocks cross freely, surfaces of the same block touch only at conical intersections. “Lowest” ranks surfaces by their minimum on the plane; “Ground states” selects every surface that is the ground state somewhere on it. Color marks the spin multiplicity; within it, shading runs from dark to light with energy on one scale for all surfaces, and the mesh lines follow the grid.

Conical intersections

Compute the surfaces to search for them.

Searched between neighboring states of the same (2S+1, Γ) block: gap minima on the grid, refined until the gap is below 10−6 B and kept only if the lower state’s eigenvector changes sign around a small loop. Markers (diamonds; open circles on the Oh axis Ds = Dt = 0) appear when both surfaces are shown; click a row to show them. Pairs degenerate over the whole plane are not cones and are skipped.
